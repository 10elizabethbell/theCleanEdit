## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking, hosted on GitHub Pages. Chosen because the site must load fast on a phone on cellular, inside Facebook's in-app browser, and be easy to hand over.

## Users

- **Primary: homeowners near Sparta, NJ** who find Jen through Facebook (a group post or her page), on a phone, often inside Facebook's in-app browser. They want to know who she is, what she does, and how to ask for an estimate, in one screen.
- **First reader: Jen herself.** The site is given to her free; it has to show her own business done better than she imagined.
- Age skew: unknown (not stated in the brief). Treated as a general homeowner audience.

## Product Purpose

A one-page website for The Clean Edit • Home + Closet that turns a text-only Facebook ad into a place customers can read about Jen's services and ask for an estimate by text or call. Success: a visitor texts Jen for an estimate.

## Positioning

One person, Jen, who both cleans and organizes: "Cleaning & Home Organization" (logo). Her page's line: "Creating spaces that feel like home." (inferred: the combination of cleaning plus closet/home organizing is the thing a plain cleaning service can't claim.)
